# InntQ. / 影拓 — Brand Identity

The final identity combines the approved Master2 symbol with the second proposal's lettering style. The English brand wordmark is exactly **InntQ.**: five letters followed by the original proposal's outlined period. Current logo artwork, presentation boards and design copy use this spelling throughout.

## Blue and black editions

Both editions contain the same nine outlined assets: Standard symbol, Micro symbol, English, Chinese, bilingual wordmarks, symbol plus English, symbol plus Chinese, horizontal bilingual lockup, and equal-width stacked bilingual lockup.

- Brand/Blue: **#0E2D4D**
- Brand/Black: **#000000**
- The two symbol bodies always share the same color.
- The stacked arrangement keeps English above Chinese, equal visible widths, and the approved fine horizontal divider.
- The I, n, n and t outlines are preserved. The uppercase Q has the same visible height as n, with an enlarged inner counter and a shorter inward tail extension. The exterior shape and 18-unit outer tail remain unchanged; the counter adjustment leaves approximately 26-unit side weights for a more open appearance. The period retains its original outline, baseline and optical spacing logic.
- Main horizontal English and Chinese logo lockups align the letter baseline with the bottom of the symbol. In the English lockup, the original period retains its small optical overshoot.
- In the symbol plus Chinese lockup, the Chinese wordmark is 15 percent larger than its original size, with uniform scaling and the same bottom alignment. Other Chinese assets retain their existing proportions.
- No descriptor is added to the Chinese brand.

## QA and minimum sizes

All 18 current native SVG and 54 transparent PNG exports pass geometry, color and crop checks against the clean sources. All six native 3200 x 2400 presentation PNGs were visually reviewed; their 82 expected text items are complete, correctly spelled and uncropped. All six native PDF pages were reviewed in blue-then-black order, with 82 text objects matching the current source and zero embedded raster images.

Current native minimum-size checks retain a readable InntQ. wordmark, visible period and open Q counter at primary symbol height 24 px and stacked symbol height 48 px. The Q counter is approximately 5.61 x 5.95 px and 5.47 x 5.80 px respectively. The stacked fine divider is 0.125 px high at its minimum size and may become faint.

**NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW — final 16 px deployment:** integer-aligned Standard and Micro symbol previews retain two bodies, but fractional pixel placement can visually join antialiased edges. Micro was more robust in the tested quarter-pixel alignments. Use Micro at 16 px and review the final rendered placement; this is a small-raster placement limitation, not contact in the vector geometry.

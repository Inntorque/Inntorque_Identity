# InntQ. / 影拓 — Brand Identity v1.1

The final identity combines the approved Master2 symbol with the second proposal's lettering style. The English brand wordmark is exactly **InntQ.**: five letters followed by the original proposal's outlined period. Current logo artwork, presentation boards and design copy use this spelling throughout.

## Blue and black editions

Both editions contain the same nine outlined assets: Standard symbol, Micro symbol, English, Chinese, bilingual wordmarks, symbol plus English, symbol plus Chinese, horizontal bilingual lockup, and equal-width stacked bilingual lockup.

- Brand/Blue: **#0E2D4D**
- Brand/Black: **#000000**
- The two symbol bodies always share the same color.
- The stacked arrangement keeps English above Chinese, equal visible widths, and the approved fine horizontal divider.
- The existing five letter outlines are preserved. The period retains the original outline, baseline and optical spacing logic.
- No descriptor is added to the Chinese brand.

## QA and minimum sizes

All 18 SVG and 54 transparent PNG exports were compared against their clean sources. Both colors preserve the same geometry, all English-containing variants contain one period, and no fonts or raster images are needed by the logo SVG files. Figma's small coordinate rounding differences are recorded in the QA reports.

All six PDF pages and native presentation PNGs were visually reviewed. All 82 expected text nodes are complete, the current abbreviated brand spelling always includes its period, and the PDF has zero embedded raster images.

At a true symbol height of 24 px, the English primary lockup including its period remains readable. The stacked bilingual lockup is readable at a symbol height of 48 px. Its intentionally thin divider is only 0.125 px high at that size and may become faint.

**NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW — final 16 px deployment:** integer-aligned Standard and Micro symbol previews retain two bodies, but fractional pixel placement can visually join antialiased edges. Micro was more robust in the tested quarter-pixel alignments. Use Micro at 16 px and review the final rendered placement; this is a small-raster placement limitation, not contact in the vector geometry.

Physical manufacturing proofs are outside this digital QA. No Illustrator file has been fabricated. The previous local v1.0 package remains available separately. No email was sent as part of this revision.
